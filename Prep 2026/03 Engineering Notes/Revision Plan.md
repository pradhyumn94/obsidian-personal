# Revision Plan

Spaced-repetition pass over the 24 notes in [[Index|Engineering Notes Index]], timed against the remaining weeks in [[Prep 2026/Roadmap|Roadmap]] (Phase 4 tail → Phase 6). Goal: Tier 1 concepts are cold-recall-ready before the heavy mock weeks (21 Sept onward); everything else gets at least one pass.

## How a pass works
Don't re-read the note first. Per concept, timebox to 5-10 min:
1. Close the note. Say or write out loud: what problem it solves, the 2-3 core mechanisms, and one failure mode / tradeoff.
2. Open the note. Diff against what you just said — note gaps directly in the note (or below, in this file's log).
3. Check it off for that pass below.

Log gaps as you find them under **Gaps Found**, don't just silently fix and move on — recurring gaps tell you a note needs rewriting, not just re-reading.
